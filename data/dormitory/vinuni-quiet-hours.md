# Noise Policy, Quiet Hours and Out/In Hours

VinUniversity expects residents to respect their neighbors and keep noise to a minimum during quiet hours.

Quiet hours are defined as follows:

- Sunday through Thursday: 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM
- Friday through Saturday: 12:00 AM to 7:00 AM

During holidays and exam weeks, adjustments may apply.

Students are also expected to follow residence rules for staying on campus or notifying the Residential Office when they need to be away for an extended period.
