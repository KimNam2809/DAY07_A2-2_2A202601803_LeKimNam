# Move In and Move Out Procedure

Students must complete the required check-in paperwork before moving into VinUniversity residence.

Check-in procedures include bringing the required identification documents, filling in the campus residence form, signing the property handover form for the apartment, and completing temporary residence registration forms.

Students are required to check the room within one day after receiving the key and report any damage or missing equipment.

For check-out, students must complete the inspection, schedule a move-out time with the Residential Office, clean the room, remove personal items and take trash or recycling to the correct area, and return access keys and borrowed items on the day of moving out.
