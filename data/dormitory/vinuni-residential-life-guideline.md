# Residential Life Guideline

This guideline applies to VinUniversity students residing in VinUni-provided accommodation, including on-campus dormitories and off-campus residences arranged by the University and its partners.

The guideline is designed to help students prepare for residential life, develop respect for others, make responsible lifestyle choices, and live independently in harmony with a diverse community.

Key topics covered in the guideline include community principles, support network, noise policy, guest visit policy, room change request, move in and move out procedure, residents' code of conduct, and health and safety.

All first-year students are required to reside in the VinUni dormitory.
