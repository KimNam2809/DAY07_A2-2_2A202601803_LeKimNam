# Residence Hall Quiet Hour Policy

This policy is meant to promote a residence hall environment where students may live, sleep, and study without disruption from noise.

Quiet hours apply to all Day Campus students residing in residence halls at Columbia College.

Quiet hours are in effect at the following times:

- Sunday through Thursday: 10:00 PM to 10:00 AM
- Friday through Saturday: 12:00 AM to 12:00 PM

Courtesy hours are in effect at all times, and students must comply when asked by a staff member or fellow student to reduce noise.
