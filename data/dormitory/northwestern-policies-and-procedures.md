# Policies and Procedures

The policies and procedures listed in this document are incorporated into the residence and board contract between student residents and Northwestern University, and all residents must comply.

The policy areas include housekeeping and safety regulations, room inspections, fire safety, room decorations, roommates, overnight guests, quiet hours and noise, room change information, moving out and damages, and many other residence-related rules.

The page also states that residents have the right to a reasonable degree of quiet and that each living unit is expected to determine its quiet-hour policy at the beginning of the academic year.
