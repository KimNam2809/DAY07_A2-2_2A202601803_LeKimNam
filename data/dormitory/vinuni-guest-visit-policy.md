# Guest Visit Policy

Residents may receive guests in VinUni-provided residential areas as long as the guests respect the privacy of other residents and follow residence policies.

Important rules include:

- Guests should be registered with the Residential Office.
- Guests should meet residents in the common area and must not enter the host apartment without prior consent from co-tenants.
- Guests must always be accompanied by the host and should not be given the apartment key.
- The host is responsible for the behavior of the guest.
- Daytime guests should stay no later than 10:00 PM.
- Each resident may have no more than three guests at the same time.
- Guest visits are not received before 7:00 AM or after 9:00 PM except in emergency cases.
